StayAwake

You're out the door. Your Mac isn't done yet.

The upload's at 80%. The build's still running. Normally, closing the lid kills it right there. Click StayAwake first, and it keeps going anyway — closed lid and all.

Late for the door, laptop half-charged, something still syncing — one click and you can just go.
You won't remember to turn it off later. It already knows that — set a timer and it turns itself off first.
The menu bar icon shows you at a glance Fills up while StayAwake is on, drains as your timer counts down

Setup

01
Open the download, drag to Applications Same as installing any other Mac app.
02
Launch StayAwake, find the cup icon It sits quietly in your menu bar — no dock icon, no window.
03
Click it, choose Keep Awake Want it to survive a closed lid too? Pick that option instead — it asks you to approve once, then never again.
