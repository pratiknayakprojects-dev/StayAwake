Carry your workstation

Heading out with your Mac in your bag? Carry My Workstation keeps it awake for an hour and reconnects to your iPhone's hotspot on its own.

Setup: open the download, drag StayAwake to Applications, then click the cup icon in your menu bar and choose Keep Awake. For closed-lid mode you approve a one-time prompt, and it never asks again.
